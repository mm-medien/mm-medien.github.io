Content Robot

Content Robot is a small private tool that publishes short videos created by its owner to the owner's own social media accounts (TikTok, Instagram, YouTube).

How it works

  1. The owner signs in with TikTok (Login Kit) and authorizes the tool for their own TikTok account.
  2. The owner prepares a finished video and a caption on their computer.
  3. Content Robot uploads the video with the Content Posting API (Direct Post) to the owner's TikTok account, with the caption and privacy level chosen by the owner, and always marks it as AI-generated content (the videos are made with AI tools).

Content Robot does not post to anyone else's accounts, does not read other users' data and does not show ads.
