Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

1. Who is responsible

Content Robot is operated by an individual developer. Contact: m.akhmedov1515@gmail.com

2. What data the tool uses

  • TikTok: basic profile information (open ID, display name, avatar) via the scope user.info.basic, to show which account is connected, and permission to publish videos via video.publish.
  • Access tokens issued by TikTok, stored only on the operator's own computer, never shared.
  • Videos and captions prepared by the account owner, sent only to the owner's own account.

3. What the tool does not do

It does not collect data about other TikTok users, does not use analytics or tracking cookies, does not sell or share any data and does not show ads. This website itself stores no cookies.

4. How long data is kept

Access tokens are kept until access is revoked; videos are kept by the owner on their own computer.

5. Your rights

You can revoke access at any time in the TikTok app (Settings → Security and permissions → Apps and services). Under the GDPR you may request information about, correction or deletion of your data by email.
